Reelta

Show your video to people already looking for you

Reelta is an app where families and couples in London ask what to do, eat or buy, and watch real short videos before they book. We'd like to show your video to people searching for what you offer, with a button straight to your booking or shop page.

How it works

  1. Send us the link of a video you've already published (Instagram, TikTok or YouTube) and the link where people book or buy.
  2. We check it. If it fits, it appears in matching searches, marked «Featured».
  3. People watch it and tap through to your page. You keep the customer and the sale.

The rules, in plain words

  • It's free. No contract, no commission from you.
  • The video stays yours. We only show the public post through the platform's own player; we never download or edit it.
  • You can ask us to remove it at any time, and we do it within 48 hours.
  • We can decline a video, for example if it doesn't fit what people search for.
  • Your video is always marked «Featured», so people know it was chosen, not found at random. No fake reviews, ever.
  • We keep your business name, email and links only to manage this, and delete them when you leave. Details in our privacy notice.
